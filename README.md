
🏦 Bank Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI

An Interactive Banking Analytics Project | Customer Behavior, Churn & Retention Analysis

---

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn in the banking sector to understand customer behavior, identify high-risk customer segments, and uncover patterns associated with customer attrition.

Using a dataset of 10,000 bank customers, I performed data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, and developed interactive dashboards using Microsoft Power BI.

The main goal is to transform raw banking data into meaningful business insights that support better decision-making and customer retention strategies.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn and retention rates.
- Identify customer segments with higher churn rates.
- Explore geographical differences in customer attrition.
- Analyze the relationship between customer age and churn.
- Investigate banking product usage and customer churn.
- Explore customer financial characteristics.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

💡 Key Insights

- Germany recorded the highest customer churn rate at approximately 32%.
- Customers aged 45–54 recorded the highest churn rate among the analyzed age groups, at 48.15%.
- Customers aged 55 and above also showed a high churn rate of 38.78%.
- Customers with 3 and 4 banking products showed significantly higher churn rates.
- Customer churn varied across tenure groups, highlighting differences in retention patterns.
- The overall customer churn rate was 20.37%, while the retention rate was 79.63%.

📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could help improve customer retention:

1. Geographical Focus: Investigate the reasons behind the high churn rate in Germany and develop targeted retention strategies.
2. Age-Based Strategies: Design personalized banking services and retention programs for customers aged 45 and above.
3. Product Optimization: Review the banking products offered to customers holding multiple products to identify potential service or satisfaction issues.
4. Customer Engagement: Strengthen engagement programs for inactive customers.
5. Continuous Monitoring: Monitor churn indicators regularly to identify high-risk customer segments.
